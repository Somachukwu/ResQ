## 🚨 1. Creative Mandate: What Is at Stake

> *"Every second between emergency detection and trauma admission decides human survival."*

ResQ is not a consumer app, a rideshare clone, or a social network. It is a pre-hospital emergency orchestration platform being presented on a world stage at the **IEEE Response Quest Challenge 2026**.

The logo you design will be judged by international IEEE emergency informatics experts, trauma surgeons, and urban disaster coordinators. More importantly, it will be viewed by:
1. **A terrified bystander** on the Enugu–Onitsha Expressway staring at an unresponsive head-injury victim on a dark roadside.
2. **An overworked dispatcher** managing high-velocity emergency queues across Enugu State.
3. **An ambulance paramedic** moving at 110 km/h in blinding rain, checking a dashboard mount.

### Your Design Objective
Your mark must communicate three things in less than **250 milliseconds**:
1. **Absolute Clinical Calm:** It lowers the user's cortisol level. It is not frantic, hysterical, or chaotic.
2. **Authoritative Intelligence:** It feels as precise as a surgical laser and as reliable as an emergency radar.
3. **Deep Human Solidarity:** It radiates the core ResQ promise: *"You are not alone. We are with you."*

## 4. Conceptual Directions to Explore

We do **not** want a generic Red Cross or a stethoscope clip-art. You are invited to explore, combine, or invent around these three conceptual pillars:

### Direction A: The Active "Q" (Lettermark + Telemetry)
* Take the capital letter **Q** and transform its signature descender/tail into an active element:
  * An **EKG vital rhythm blip** resolving into a stable line.
  * A **directional GIS vector/compass needle** pointing toward safety.
  * A **beacon pulse / radar sweep** indicating live satellite connection.
* **Why it works:** Keeps the brand instantly readable as "ResQ", while infusing the mark with clinical motion and technological precision.

### Direction B: The ResQ Triad (The 3 Synchronized Actors)
* ResQ is built around the unbreakable coordination of **Three Actors**:
  1. *Civilian / Bystander* (The scene reporter)
  2. *Dispatcher* (The central coordinator)
  3. *Responder* (The ambulance / field paramedic)
* Visual metaphor: Three clean geometric nodes, an interlocking triquetra, or a continuous geometric loop forming a stylized "R" or shield.
* **Why it works:** Directly represents the technical innovation that wins the IEEE competition—the seamless, real-time synchronization of scene, dispatch, and hospital.

### Direction C: The Safe Coordinate (Crosshair + Life Pulse)
* Combining the **GIS location coordinate** (finding the accident on unmapped Nigerian highways) with the **vital life sign** (medical recovery).
* Subtle intersection of a medical cross / radar target, executed with minimalist modernist geometry (rounded terminals, optical offsets).
* **Why it works:** Bridges the technical GIS engineering with humane emergency medicine.

|---|---|---|---|
| **ResQ Teal** | `#0D6E6E` | `rgb(13, 110, 110)` | **Primary Brand Color.** Conveys clinical authority, calm, life, and trust. Must dominate the mark (~80%). |

> ⚠️ **Strict Color Rule:** The logo must be designed in **Teal (`#0D6E6E`) as the primary tone** with optional **Amber (`#F0920A`) accent**. Never use decorative rainbow gradients, magenta, neon cyan, or arbitrary colors.

## 6. Typography & Wordmark Pairing

When designing the standalone wordmark (`ResQ`) or lockup:
* **Base Geometric Structure:** Clean, modern, open geometric sans-serif (consistent with **Inter** and **DM Sans**).
* **Weights:** Strict adherence to **Medium (500) and Regular (400)**. No aggressive black/heavy bold weights that generate visual tension.
* **Kerning & Tracking:** Generous, breathable letter-spacing (+0.02em to +0.05em). The letters must never feel cramped.
* **Hierarchy:** The capital **Q** can have a distinct visual character, but must optically balance with the **R**, **e**, and **s**.

## 7. Scaling, Contexts & Physical Applications

The logo must perform impeccably across extreme scale variations:

```
[16px Favicon]  ──►  [32px App Header]  ──►  [180px App Icon]  ──►  [Ambulance Door / Uniform Patch]
  Must be 100%         Crisp outline           Tactile depth &           High-vis reflective,
  distinguishable      beside text             subtle geometry           single-color stamp
```

### Context Checklist:
1. **The 16×16px Browser Favicon:** Must remain completely recognizable without turning into an unreadable smudge.
2. **Mobile Screen Header (28px height):** Must lock up cleanly alongside actor tags (e.g., `ResQ · civilian`, `ResQ · dispatch`, `ResQ · AMB-07`).
3. **High-Glare Midday Sunlight:** Must maintain high-contrast legibility on cheap LCD smartphone screens held outdoors in 35°C Nigerian sun.
4. **OLED Dark Mode:** Must shine cleanly on `#0F1117` and `#1A1A1A` dark canvases without awkward color bleeding.
5. **Physical Livery & Apparel:** Must translate cleanly to:
   - High-visibility reflective vinyl on ambulance doors.
   - Embroidered patches on first responder tactical vests.
   - Screen-printed lanyard badges for IEEE competition judges.

## 8. Strict Design Rules: DOs and DON'Ts

### ✅ DO:
* **DO** create a logo that works equally well in **pure monochrome** (100% white on black, 100% black on white).
* **DO** prioritize clean, mathematical geometry with generous negative space.
* **DO** make the mark feel fast, responsive, and lifesaving.
* **DO** use consistent stroke weights (if using linear geometry, standard `1.5px` or `2.0px` optical ratios).
* **DO** provide both a **standalone icon/symbol** (for app icons, avatars, vehicle badges) and a **horizontal lockup** (symbol + wordmark).

### ❌ STRICTLY DO NOT:
* **DO NOT** use the exact Red Cross / Red Crescent emblem (strictly protected by international humanitarian law and Geneva Conventions).
* **DO NOT** draw literal, cliché emergency illustrations (no cartoon ambulances, sirens with noise lines, band-aids, or stethoscopes).
* **DO NOT** use trendy "AI sparkles" (✨), tech hexagons, or blockchain cubes. ResQ is a serious medical infrastructure platform.
* **DO NOT** use skeuomorphic 3D glass, heavy glossy bevels, or drop shadows that cannot be reproduced cleanly in SVG.
* **DO NOT** make the design aggressive, militaristic, or panic-inducing (no barbed lightning bolts or radioactive warning triangles).
